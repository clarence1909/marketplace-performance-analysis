# CV Bullet Options

Three options for the same project — pick whichever fits the rest of your
CV's tone, or mix phrases from more than one. Replace every `[X]` with the
real figure once you've run `sql/04_analysis.sql` against the loaded
dataset — don't estimate or round from memory; pull it from the query
output.

**1. SQL / data modeling emphasis**

> Modeled an 8-table e-commerce dataset (~100K orders) in PostgreSQL,
> writing CTE- and window-function-based SQL to resolve order/item/
> payment/review granularity mismatches and produce an order-level fact
> view; identified and quantified [X] data-integrity issues (orphaned
> keys, duplicate records) before analysis.

**2. Dashboard / insight emphasis**

> Built a 4-page Power BI dashboard (6 DAX measures) on a Brazilian
> marketplace dataset, surfacing that late-delivered orders scored [X]
> points lower on average review than on-time orders across [Y] orders
> analyzed — translated into a seller monitoring recommendation.

**3. Data-quality / benchmarking emphasis**

> Ran a full data-integrity audit (row counts, duplicate, orphan-key, and
> granularity checks) on a 100K-row e-commerce dataset, then benchmarked
> the top 10% of sellers by GMV against the remaining 90%, finding a
> [X]pp gap in late-delivery rate — used to recommend a seller-tiering
> approach.

**Notes on using these**

- Keep whichever bullet you use to one line if your CV format is dense —
  these are written slightly long so you have material to trim rather
  than pad.
- If you're tailoring for the Shopee Malaysia BI Executive role
  specifically, bullet 2 or 3 map most directly to the job description's
  language ("turning analysis into business actions", "benchmarking").
- Don't combine all three into one bullet — pick the angle that's least
  duplicated elsewhere on your CV (e.g. if another bullet already covers
  your SQL/Python data-pipeline work from Parexel, lead with 2 or 3
  here instead of 1).
